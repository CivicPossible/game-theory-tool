How Scoring Worked

🌊 All to the Coast!

Each group earns 1 point.

⛰️ All to the Mountains!

Each group loses 1 point.

Mixed Choices

The side with fewer groups earns points equal to the number of groups on the other side.

The side with more groups loses points equal to the number of groups on the other side.

If tied, Mountains earns and Coast loses points equal to half the total groups.
